# MONITORING AND EVALUATION TOOLS[cite: 187]

The Social Development Unit (SDU) offices employ diverse monitoring and evaluation (M&E) tools for their programs and projects.[cite: 187] The unit also utilizes monthly and quarterly reports as part of its M&E mechanisms.[cite: 187] These mechanisms are crucial to ensure that office programs and projects align with both the unit's and the university's vision and mission.[cite: 187] Key M&E tools employed across the SDU include:[cite: 187]

1. Strategic Plan Periodic Update Template[cite: 187]
2. Strategic Plan Templates/Matrices:[cite: 187]
   a. Target Performance Matrix[cite: 187]
   b. Operational Plan Matrix[cite: 187]
   c. Activity Implementation Monitoring Map[cite: 187]
   d. Plan Evaluation Matrix[cite: 187]
3. Client Satisfaction Rate Survey[cite: 187]
4. University Program/Project Design Template[cite: 187]
5. Guidelines for Externally-Funded Projects[cite: 187]
