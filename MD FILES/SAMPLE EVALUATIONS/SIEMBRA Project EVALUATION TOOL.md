This evaluation tool is designed to systematically assess various aspects of a tree-growing project, from site preparation to community involvement and sustainability.[cite: 184] It consists of key indicators and guiding questions to ensure a comprehensive review of the project's implementation and long-term impact.[cite: 184]

This evaluation tool uses a 4-point rating scale to assess the effectiveness and sustainability of the tree-growing project.[cite: 184] Please read each question carefully and assign a score based on actual observations, data, and feedback.[cite: 184]

## Understanding the Rating Scale[cite: 184]
Each question should be rated using the following scale:[cite: 184]
* 4- Excellent: The criterion is fully met, with outstanding performance and no need for improvement.[cite: 184] Best practices are observed, and sustainability measures are well-established.[cite: 184]
* 3 - Very Good: The criterion is mostly met, with minor areas for improvement.[cite: 184] Performance is strong, and the project is generally well-managed.[cite: 184]
* 2- Good: The criterion is somewhat met, but there are noticeable gaps.[cite: 184] Some improvements are needed to enhance effectiveness and sustainability.[cite: 184]
* 1 - Poor: The criterion is not met or is significantly lacking.[cite: 184] Major issues or challenges exist that require immediate corrective action.[cite: 184]
