They said capacity building should focus on the people, meaning the trainings staff attended, facilitated, organized, or spoke at, and the matching of staff to trainings by skill. They felt we had added areas with their own rules, especially budgeting, that are separate from that focus. They also said our 16 objectives were mostly individual features and should be grouped into a few major functions.

The panel also raised questions we want to confirm with you today:

- how many staff there are
- what the audit form is for
- whether the Director and Secretary have the same role
- how staff are rated
- what skills each office needs

PART 2: WHAT WE ARE REMOVING OR CHANGING (say this second, about 2 minutes)

Removed:

- All budget functions. That means allocation, sub-distribution to staff, expense logging, receipts, and overspend alerts.

Changed:

- The 16 objectives will be merged into three major functions: the staff training profile, training management, and reports on staff competencies and training needs. Training management covers creating trainings, assigning staff by matching skills, and evaluating the training and the roles people played in it.
- The title will change to focus on trainings conducted by SDU offices and trainings attended by staff.
- Knowledge/expertise will be separated from skills, and set per office.
- The process flow will be redrawn with swimlanes for each actor, and the DFD and ERD will be redone for the smaller scope.
- We will add a table mapping each problem to its proposed solution. 1.

SHARIFF QUESTIONS:

Roles and users

1. The Director and Secretary: what can each one do that the other cannot? The panel looked at your org chart and doubted they have the same role.

2. Should the Director be in the pool of trainers, facilitators, and speakers who can be assigned to trainings? What about Office Heads? You said before that the Director doesn't need her own training log, but the panel asked about the trainer pool specifically.

3. What is the audit form used for, and is it one form for all six offices? Should the staff profile in our system replace it? Which of its fields should we keep?

Participation roles

4. We have participant, facilitator, speaker, and organizer. Is that complete? Do you also use resource person, host or emcee, or documenter? How do you define each one, for example organizer as the person in charge of logistics?

5. The panel suggested showing capacity as counts, such as how many sessions someone facilitated per topic, with a bar chart per person. Would that be an acceptable basis, even though it counts experience and not quality of performance?

6. Do you want trainings staff attended outside SDU recorded too, including partner-sponsored ones? Do you want the trainings SDU offices conduct recorded as their own records, with title, date, venue, and category?

Matching and assignment

7. Walk us through the last time you chose who would attend a training or answer an external request. What information did you look at?

8. When you pick someone, are you matching their strongest skill, or sending someone who lacks a skill so they can develop it? Or both?

9. When the Director assigns a staff member from another center, does that staff member's own Office Head need to be told or approve?

Reports and decisions

10. What are the first three reports you would want from this system, for example staff with no training this year or offices with no recorded facilitator?

11. Is there a minimum number of trainings per staff member per year, or required topics per office? What would count as too many for one person? The panel asked what a "gap" and "over-participation" actually mean.

12. How do you identify a staff member's training needs now?

Scope checks

13. Partner institutions: do you need to track agreements and expiry dates, or only note which partner sponsored a training? If we keep the module, should Office Heads edit their own office's records? The paper's test case said read-only, and a panelist asked if that was right.

14. In-system messaging: do you need it, or are notifications enough? What do you use now, such as email or Messenger?

Documents to ask for

15. Do you have job descriptions, a list of required competencies, or a staff list per office that we can cite?
